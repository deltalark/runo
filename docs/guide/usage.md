Tokens, cost and context use are shown per turn and per session, and today's total in the status bar.

**Settings → Safety** sets a spending limit per session and per day, how many agents may run at once, and when a quiet agent gets flagged. It also holds what agents may do without asking and the confirmations before deleting.
