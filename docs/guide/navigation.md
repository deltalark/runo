The rail on the left switches between Home ({go-home}), Sessions ({go-sessions}) and Git ({go-review}). Playbooks, History and Notes sit in the ⋯ menu at the foot of the rail, next to Settings ({settings}); pin any of them, or the Inbox, out on the rail in **Settings → General → Rail**. The current project's Design ({go-design}) and Architecture ({go-architecture}) sit under its badge. The Inbox also opens from the bell in the top bar.

{sidebar} shows or hides the sidebar. Right-click any session ({context-menu}) to pin, rename or archive it ({archive}). {stop} stops the running agent.
