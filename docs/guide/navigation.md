The rail on the left switches between Home ({go-home}), Sessions ({go-sessions}), Review, GitHub, Playbooks and History, then Design ({go-design}) and Architecture ({go-architecture}), with Notes and Settings ({settings}) at the bottom.

{sidebar} shows or hides the sidebar. Right-click any session ({context-menu}) to pin, rename or archive it ({archive}). {stop} stops the running agent.
