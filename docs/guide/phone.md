Runo's mobile app follows every session and lets you answer it from your phone.

1. In **Settings → Phone**, switch on **Phone bridge**.
2. Press **Pair a new phone** and scan the QR code with the Runo mobile app.

By default the bridge only listens on your Tailscale network or this computer, never on a shared network.
