**Files** lets you browse and edit the worktree, with syntax highlighting, rendered Markdown, images and CSV tables. {save-file} saves.

**Terminal** is a real shell in the session's folder, with several tabs. Your quick commands (Settings → General → Quick commands) appear as one-click buttons.
