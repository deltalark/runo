The Arcade has three small games to play while agents work: **Spark** (Space or click to lift a spark through the gates), **Commits** (a snake that grows on commits; arrows to steer) and **Merge** (2048; arrows to slide). Open it with **Open the Arcade** in Quick open, or the **Play while you wait** chip beside "Working" while an agent runs.

Space starts and restarts a round. Closing the Arcade pauses the game, so a round picks up where it left off, and each game keeps its best score. It says when your agents are done, with **Back to work**.
