On a Mac, Runo puts a glyph in the menu bar by the clock. The number beside it counts the sessions that need you, or the agents working when nothing does, and a dot means something is waiting on you.

Click it to see each session that needs you or is working, with its agent, project and how long it has run. Hover a session for its branch, last reply, changes and cost, with **Open session** and **Ask Runo about this…**. **Ask Runo…** opens the assistant.

Turn it off in **Settings → General → Alerts**. Windows and Linux don't have it yet.
