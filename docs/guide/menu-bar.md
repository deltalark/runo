On a Mac, Runo puts a glyph in the menu bar by the clock. The number beside it counts the sessions that need you, or the agents working when nothing does, and a dot means something is waiting on you.

Click it for a panel with each session that needs you or is working. Allow, Always or Deny a waiting permission and Stop a working agent right there, or click a session for its branch, last reply, changes and cost, and Open it in Runo. **Ask Runo** at the bottom is the same chat as the assistant in the window.

With the menu bar on, ⌘Q and closing the window hide Runo to the menu bar and your agents keep working; **Quit Runo** in the panel quits. Turn either off in **Settings → General → Alerts**. Windows and Linux don't have it yet.
