The Runo mark in the corner answers questions about Runo. Press {ask-runo} or click it, then ask how to do something.

Turn it on or off in **Settings → General → Runo assistant**. It answers with your default agent on a fast model and never changes anything; with **Guide only** it answers from this guide without an agent.
