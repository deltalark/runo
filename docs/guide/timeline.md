The Timeline lists what happened across your projects, newest first and grouped by day: sessions started, finished, failed or stopped, commits, pushes, updates, undone turns, discarded changes and scheduled playbook runs. Click a row to open its session. Open it with **Go to Timeline** in Quick open.
