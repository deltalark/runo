Send one task to several agents at once. Each gets its own worktree, so they never touch each other's files.

1. Open **New session** ({new-session}) and write the task.
2. Click **Race several agents** and pick two or more agents.
3. When they finish, compare the results and click **Keep this one** on the best. Runo stops the others and deletes their worktrees and branches.
