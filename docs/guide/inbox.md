The Inbox ({go-inbox}, or the bell in the top bar) is one list of everything waiting on you, across projects. Blocked agents come first with their answer inline (**Allow**, **Approve** a plan, or pick an option), then sessions that finished or failed while you weren't looking, then review requests and failed checks from GitHub. Pin it out on the rail in **Settings → General → Rail**.
