Design ({go-design}, under the project's badge on the rail) is a design canvas for the current project. Screens are real HTML kept in the project's `design/` folder (`screens/`, `tokens.css` for the theme, `components.css`, `prototype.js` for links), drawn by WebKit on a Mac and a headless Chromium elsewhere, so what you approve is what gets built.

Build by hand or with AI. The tool bar at the bottom of the canvas has the tools and the **Design**, **Prototype** and **Code** modes; layers are on the left, and the right panel has typed fields for the picked layer, the theme's variables, styles and the checks. Type in the ask box (press `/`) to have an agent change the screen; a quick ask about one layer lands as a single edit, and asks typed while AI works wait in line. **Try options** draws three takes on a layer side by side to pick from. With the marker (M) you circle or scribble on screens to show AI what you mean.

Keys on the canvas: V select, H hand (or hold Space), R rectangle, O ellipse, L line, Shift+L arrow, P pen, T text, F add a screen, C comment, M marker, I eyedropper, Shift+A auto layout, Shift+G layout grids. ⌘G groups and ⇧⌘G ungroups, ⌥⌘K makes a component, ⌘R renames, ⇧⌘L locks, ⇧⌘H hides, ⌘D duplicates, ⌘C/⌘X/⌘V copy, cut and paste, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, Delete removes, arrows nudge (Shift for 10), Enter edits text or steps in and Shift+Enter steps out, Tab walks the layers. Shift+1 fits, Shift+2 zooms to the selection, Shift+0 is 100%, ⌘+ and ⌘− zoom. ⌥⌘Enter plays the prototype. On Windows and Linux use Ctrl for ⌘.

Components keep a piece once and reuse it as instances with variants; pushing a change updates every instance. Libraries publish components to other projects. Comments pin notes with replies to a point on a screen, and mentioning AI hands a comment to it to fix. **History** keeps versions, saved on their own and by name, to compare and put back. **Present** plays the prototype. **Hand off** gives each layer a lasting id, saves a version and writes a spec, pictures and the tokens (DTCG JSON, Tailwind and the mobile stack's theme) to `design/handoff/`, and the **Code** tab writes a layer or screen as CSS, React or SwiftUI. Export a screen or a part of it as a picture or a standalone page. Import frames from Figma with a link once a **Figma access token** is set in **Settings → General → Design**.
