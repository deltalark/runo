Open Settings with {settings}, the gear at the foot of the rail, **Runo → Settings…** in the menu bar, or **Open settings** in Quick open. It opens on **Overview**: a card per area with what stands out in it, and every on/off switch in one grid to flip right there.

The tabs across the top hold the pages: **General** (and **Worktrees**), **Look** (**Appearance** and **Terminal**), **Agents** (**Providers**, each agent's own page, **Chat**, **MCP**, **Skills** and **Hooks**), **Connections** (and **Phone**), **Safety** and **Keys** (**Keybindings**). The search box finds any setting by its name or description across every page.

Every change saves straight away. The assistant knows every setting and what each is set to now, and can change a switch or a choice, or press a button such as **Check now**, when you ask; it shows a card first and nothing changes until you click it.
