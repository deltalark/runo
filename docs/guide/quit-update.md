To quit Runo, press ⌘Q on a Mac (Ctrl+Q elsewhere), pick **Runo → Quit Runo** in the menu bar, or run **Quit Runo** from Quick open. The assistant can quit it for you too: ask it to, and click **Quit Runo** on the card.

If agents are running on this computer, the first quit only warns that quitting stops them; quit again within a few seconds to go ahead. Agents running on a connection (a Cove VM or your own server) keep going without Runo, and turns started here that outlive a crash are picked up again on the next launch.

On a Mac with **Show Runo in the menu bar** and **Keep running in the menu bar** on, ⌘Q and closing the window only hide Runo to the menu bar so your agents keep working. To quit for real, click the Runo glyph by the clock and pick **Quit Runo** at the bottom of the panel, or ask the assistant. Both switches are in **Settings → General → Alerts**.

Runo looks for a newer release on GitHub when it starts and every few hours. When one is out, a card offers **Download** and **What's new**; once downloaded, **Open** it and replace Runo to finish, and your sessions stay. Nothing installs without you. **Settings → General → Updates** has **Check for Runo updates** to turn the checks off, **Check now** to look straight away (it also shows which version you have), and **Show** for the logs and crash reports.
