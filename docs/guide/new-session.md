A session is one agent working on one task in one project.

1. Press {new-session} or click **New session** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Describe the task, pick the agent and the project.
3. Choose whether it works in an isolated worktree or right in your checkout, then start it.

The agent's steps stream into the chat as it works, and its changes show up under **Changes**.
