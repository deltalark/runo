Home ({go-home}) is where work starts: one prompt to start a session from, which also searches sessions, projects, playbooks and past conversations as you type. Below it are ideas to start from (such as **Find bugs**, **Add missing tests** or **Review my changes**), what's waiting on you in one line, and the sessions to pick up again. In a Crew project the prompt goes to Crew, and Home shows what Crew last said, the decisions its threads wait on, and the threads.
