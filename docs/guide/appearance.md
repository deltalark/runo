**Settings → Appearance** sets light or dark, the accent colour, a background, colourblind-friendly diff colours, the interface font and an optional glass look.

**Background** lays Aurora on the window: soft light in your accent from the top-left corner, with violet and ember from the others. It lights the frame round your work (the top bar, the rail and the status bar), and with glass on it glows through the panels as well. It takes the accent and theme you pick.
