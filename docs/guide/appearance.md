**Settings → Appearance** sets light or dark, the accent colour, a background, colourblind-friendly diff colours, the interface font and an optional glass look.

**Background** lays a fine pattern behind your work, warming into your accent toward the top-left corner: Dot matrix, Grid, Blueprint, Crosses, Rings or Stitches. Three switches pick where it lies: on the content (the page and the terminal, on by default), on the side panels (the sidebar, Changes and Files), and on the frame (the top bar, the rail and the status bar). With glass on and all three areas on, it also shows through the panels. It takes the accent and theme you pick.
