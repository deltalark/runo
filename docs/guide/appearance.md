**Settings → Appearance** sets light or dark, the accent colour, a background, colourblind-friendly diff colours, the interface font and an optional glass look.

**Background** lays a dot matrix on the window: a fine grid of dots on the frame round your work (the top bar, the rail and the status bar), warming into your accent toward the top-left corner. With glass on it shows through the panels as well. It takes the accent and theme you pick.
